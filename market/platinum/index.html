Market context

Platinum market context

Platinum is both a precious metal and an industrial input, with supply and automotive demand influencing its market. Use the reference chart for context and keep the purity, weight, and product form of each holding separate.

Dealer spreads can be wider for platinum than for frequently traded gold products, and availability varies by market. The chart is a neutral reference; it does not include fabrication, delivery, or resale conditions.

The tax treatment of physical platinum is jurisdiction-specific and product-dependent. Keep a complete cost basis and sale trail, and obtain local advice before relying on a tax assumption.
