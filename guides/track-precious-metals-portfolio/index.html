Track Precious Metals in Your Portfolio: App or Spreadsheet?

Owning Gold and Silver is one thing. Keeping purchase prices, fine weights, and today’s picture understandable is another. Both methods work; the better choice is the one that fits your routine.

2026-08-08 · MyGoldFolio

For a few stable purchases, a spreadsheet can be enough. When positions, price checks, or possible sales become regular, a dedicated app mainly saves routine work—not thinking.

What is worth recording

A precious-metals portfolio is more than one total. Record each purchase or lot clearly so you can still understand it later, whether it is a bar, coin, jewellery, or another physical holding.

  • What you hold: metal, description, and purity where useful.
  • How much: quantity and fine weight, not only gross weight.
  • When you bought it: purchase date keeps the sequence and holding period clear.
  • What it really cost: total cost including premiums, delivery, and other fees.
  • Where it is stored: only a neutral note if useful; avoid sensitive details in an unprotected file.

When a spreadsheet is enough

A spreadsheet is a sound starting point when you hold only a few positions, buy or sell rarely, and are happy to maintain formulas yourself. It can also sit well beside invoices and other records.

Treat it as a maintained record rather than a one-off note: use clear columns, one weight convention, and a backup you control.

When an app becomes useful

The benefit is rarely one number. It is the recurring questions: How is this exact purchase doing? What would a certain sale price mean? Which holding is closer to break-even? Did I include all costs?

A tracking app does not make the decision for you. It makes the information behind that decision easier to read. That structure becomes valuable with mixed Gold and Silver holdings, different purchase dates, or more than one display currency.

A simple way to decide

  1. Will your holdings remain small and mostly unchanged? A spreadsheet may be sufficient.
  2. Do you genuinely update prices regularly? If not, a spreadsheet’s current value becomes historical quickly.
  3. Do you want to understand each purchase rather than only one total? Then position-based tracking helps.

The practical conclusion

A spreadsheet is flexible; an app is structured. There is no reason to replace a simple system that still serves you. When upkeep starts costing more time than it saves, dedicated tracking can bring clarity back.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth setting up an app and entering my existing holdings?

Yes, if you want more than one total. Once each item is recorded, you can see the overall portfolio and how individual purchases are doing, filter positions, explore scenarios, and set watches or alerts. You configure it once, then let it keep watch; holdings remain private on your phone.

Do I need to keep prices and calculations up to date by hand?

No formulas need rebuilding. MyGoldFolio uses current reference-price updates for valuation and can watch the price conditions you choose. You only add or change your own transaction details when something in your portfolio changes.

Are my portfolio details private, and can I move them to another phone?

Your holdings stay on your device; there is no portfolio account or server copy. Export a backup when you choose, then import it on another device. That gives you control, but also means you should keep a safe backup.
